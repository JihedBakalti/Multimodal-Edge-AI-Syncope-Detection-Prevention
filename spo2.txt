Perfusion failure =
👉 brain doesn’t get enough blood → not enough oxygen delivery → you faint


Pre-syncope symptoms often appear when:
SpO₂ < 85%

Risk zone:
rapid drop ≥ 3–5% in short time
continuous downward trend

Oxygen delivery = blood flow × oxygen content

Stage 1 (pre-syncope):
slow decline
subtle
Stage 2 (collapse):
fast drop
severe

⚠️ WARNING PATTERN
baseline → gradual continuous decline

🚨 PRE-SYNCOPE PATTERN
baseline → gradual decline → no recovery

🔥 HIGH RISK PATTERN
baseline → gradual decline → sudden sharp drop


Oxygen stays stable at first
Then starts decreasing after ~10 seconds
Then continues progressively declining

if (
    gradual_decline and
    no_recovery
):
    risk = "⚠️ pre-syncope"

elif (
    gradual_decline and
    sudden_acceleration
):
    risk = "🚨 high risk"

elif (
    small_drop and recovery
):
    risk = "✅ normal"
